Welcome to North Star Bakery

North Star Bakery is a neighborhood bakery offering fresh breads, pastries, and custom cakes made with care. We welcome local families, commuters, and event planners looking for handmade baked goods and friendly service.

What We're Known For

Our bakery is known for naturally fermented breads, seasonal pastries, and custom cakes for birthdays, celebrations, and community events. Every item is prepared in small batches for freshness, quality, and homemade flavor.

  • Fresh artisan breads
  • Handmade pastries
  • Custom celebration cakes
  • Friendly neighborhood service
