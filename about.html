Our Story

North Star Bakery began as a small neighborhood business created to bring fresh, handmade baked goods to the local community. What started with a few family recipes has grown into a welcoming bakery serving commuters, families, and event planners.

We believe that baked goods can bring people together. Our team prepares breads, pastries, and cakes with care so every customer can enjoy something fresh and memorable.

Our Sourcing Philosophy

We select ingredients based on freshness, quality, and reliability. Whenever possible, we work with local and regional suppliers to support nearby farms and businesses.

Our flour, fruit, dairy products, herbs, and other ingredients are chosen carefully to help us create flavorful baked goods. We also plan seasonal menu items around ingredients that are available at their best.

Small-Batch Baking

Preparing products in small batches helps our team maintain freshness, reduce unnecessary waste, and give each item careful attention.

Meet Our Team

Elena leads the bread program and specializes in naturally fermented sourdough. She enjoys teaching the team traditional baking techniques and developing seasonal recipes.

Marcus prepares croissants, fruit pastries, muffins, and other morning favorites. His work focuses on balanced flavors and careful presentation.

Sofia Bennett, Cake Decorator
